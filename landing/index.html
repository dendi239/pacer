ESP32-S3 · u-blox M10 · micro-SD

Mounted where you already look.

The receiver fixes your position 25 times a second. Every fix is timed on the device and written to the card for later.

The main screen

Red is slower. Green is faster.

The big number is your gap to the session-best lap right now. Under it is the lap time you'll set if the rest of the lap matches it.

What it's made of

Off-the-shelf parts and open firmware. No PSRAM needed: the timing state fits in about 40 KB.

How a session goes

Nothing to press. Power it on and drive out of the pits.

  1. Power onThe screen comes up, the card mounts and the receiver is configured. The display reads "waiting for gps fix".
  2. First fixspeedor loads the track whose start line is closest and arms the timing.
  3. Cross the start lineLap 1 and the session clock start together, so the out lap stays off the clock.
  4. DriveEvery gate updates the delta. Crossings below walking pace are ignored, so sitting on the grid doesn't count as a lap.
