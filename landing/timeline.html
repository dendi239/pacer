Timeline

Go through a session after you've driven it. Timeline opens speedor logs and GoPro footage, splits them into laps and sectors, and puts any two laps side by side.

Download from GitHub Releases timeline-macos-arm64.zip · Apple silicon · unsigned build

What it does

  • SourcesReads the .dat logs speedor writes to its SD card and the GPS track inside GoPro video files.
  • LapsA sortable lap table per source, with sector times from the same track files the device uses.
  • DeltaThe gap between two laps at every point of the circuit, so you can see where the time went.
  • ComparisonsPick laps from the same session or different ones and compare them directly.
  • SessionsSave what you have open and pick it up again later.

First launch

The build is not signed, so macOS quarantines it. After unzipping, clear the flag once, or right-click the app and choose Open.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine timeline.app

Other platforms

Timeline builds from source with CMake on anything the rest of pacer builds on. The README has the steps.
